The app signs in to Leumi and Max, adds up every card charge that hasn't left your account yet, and takes it off your balance.

Pending purchases count too. Later installments and foreign-currency charges are shown on the side, converted at the Bank of Israel rate.

Everything around that number.

Four tabs, like the questions you actually ask about your money.

Home

  • Days until the Max debit and what that leaves per day
  • Latest payments and Leumi movements, with transfer details
  • Your European accounts, in their own section

Charges

  • The next debit by category, tap a slice for its purchases
  • Every upcoming debit, installments included
  • Search by merchant or category

Spending

  • Any period, as a running total you can scrub
  • Each merchant's history, notes and tags on purchases
  • Export to CSV for Numbers or Excel

Insights

  • Monthly budgets per category, with alerts at 80% and 100%
  • Subscriptions, salary and regular debits, found for you
  • This month against last month, and your net worth

It runs on your iPhone. Only there.

There is no True Balance server. The app does what you would do in Safari, by itself.

  • It signs in to Leumi and Max on the phone, in a hidden browser
  • Your credentials stay in this iPhone's Keychain, never synced
  • Face ID lock and a switch to hide every amount
  • Open source: read the code on GitHub

Your European accounts too.

Hello Bank, Revolut, BNP Paribas, N26 and about 2,700 other banks in 29 countries, read through open banking with Enable Banking. Read only, free, shown apart from the true balance with their shekel equivalent.

Install it in a few minutes.

True Balance isn't on the App Store. AltStore and SideStore install it with your own Apple ID and keep it up to date.

  1. Install AltStore or SideStore on your iPhoneA free Apple ID is enough.
  2. Add the True Balance sourceWith the buttons above, or by pasting this address in Sources.
  3. Install True Balance, open it and sign in to Leumi and MaxOr try it first with sample data.
